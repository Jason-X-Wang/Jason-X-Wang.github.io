I am delighted and honored to accept the invitation from the Co-Editors-in-Chief of the ***International Journal of Operations & Production Management* (IJOPM)**—Prof. Hugo Lam, Prof. Jens Roehrich, and Prof. Martin Spring—to participate in the **2027 IJOPM Workshop**, taking place on **4–5 January 2027** at **Zhejiang University** in Hangzhou, China.

Co-organized by *IJOPM*, the **School of Management at Zhejiang University (ZJU-SOM)**, the **Zhejiang Key Laboratory of Decision Intelligence (ZDI)**, and the *Journal of Digital Management (JDM)* under the theme **“Developing the Next Generation of OSCM Scholarship,”** the workshop is dedicated to supporting empirical operations and supply chain management (OSCM) scholars in strengthening the positioning, rigor, and impact of their research.

### My Roles at the 2027 IJOPM Workshop

Representing the **IJOPM Editorial Review Board (ERB)** and the **Organizing Committee**, I will contribute to the workshop across three core areas:

* **Mentoring Session Mentor:** Serving as a mentor during the dedicated *IJOPM Mentoring Session*, paired with early career researchers and PhD students to provide tailored research advice, discuss empirical study design, and guide manuscript development for top-tier OSCM outlets.
* **Parallel Presentation Session Chair:** Chairing a parallel paper presentation session and providing constructive, developmental feedback and editorial suggestions to presenting authors on their extended abstracts and full papers.
* **Organizing Committee & ERB Support:** Supporting the workshop programme and call for submissions to foster international scholarly exchange and attract high-quality empirical OSCM submissions.

### Workshop Highlights

Over the two-day programme, the IJOPM Editorial Team and leading OSCM editors will engage with attendees through:
* **IJOPM Introduction & Special Issue Sessions:** Insights into recent journal developments and upcoming Special Issue calls.
* **Editor Panel Discussions:** Practical guidance on developing and positioning high-impact empirical research papers.
* **IJOPM Mentoring & Parallel Presentation Sessions:** Direct developmental feedback from IJOPM Editors and Editorial Review Board members, alongside consideration of full papers for the **IJOPM Best Paper Awards**.
